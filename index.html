About SafeDose AI

Project Purpose

SafeDose AI is a demonstration prototype created for the USPF Changemaker Challenge. It explores how AI-assisted workflows could support medication verification, injection safety, and monitoring in resource-constrained Nigerian healthcare settings.

Limitations

  • • Not a clinically validated medical device.
  • • Not deployed in any real hospital.
  • • No real patient data is stored or processed.
  • • Does not prescribe, diagnose, or calculate doses.
